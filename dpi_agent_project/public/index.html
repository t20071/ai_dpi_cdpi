  • Credit score of at least 500 for a medium or low risk tier

  • Permission: mifos_credit:read
  • Purpose: Credit Assessment, Micro-Loan Processing or Subsidy Screening

What happens

  • Credit is a high-impact decision, so it needs higher confidence
  • High-risk or uncertain cases always go to an officer

Common questions

Does an AI decide whether I get the subsidy?

No. The AI only works out which service you are asking for and which ID you gave. Eligibility is decided by published rules, and anything uncertain goes to an officer.

What happens if I have not given consent?

Your records are not read at all. You are told which permission is missing so you can grant it in your consent manager and try again.

Can I write in Hindi?

Yes. Requests in Hindi and Hinglish are understood. The website itself is in English for now.

Is this a real government service?

No. This is a prototype that uses synthetic data to show how AI can be used safely on top of digital public infrastructure.

How it works

The AI proposes. The workflow decides. A human handles anything uncertain.

From your words to a decision

  1. Understand

    A language model, or the keyword rules, reads your message and fills in a fixed form: which service, which ID, and how sure it is. It cannot do anything else.

  2. Safety checks

    The ID must be one you actually typed. Independent keyword rules must agree with the AI about the service. Otherwise an officer takes over, before any record is opened.

  3. Identity and consent

    Your identity must be active. Your consent must be active, unexpired, for this purpose, include the right permission and carry a valid signature.

  4. Rules and risk

    Published eligibility rules are applied. A risk gate checks the AI's confidence (0.80, or 0.90 for credit) and looks for warning signs.

  5. Record

    Approved requests are written to the existing records system without changing it. Every step, approved or not, goes into a tamper-evident audit log.

The five consent gates

  1. Status: only active consent counts; revoked or expired consent is refused.
  2. Expiry: the consent must still be valid today.
  3. Permission: it must allow the exact data needed.
  4. Purpose: it must have been given for this kind of service.
  5. Signature: the record must not have been edited.

When an officer steps in

  • The AI and the rules disagree, or neither is sure.
  • The request names more than one ID, or an ID you did not type.
  • The identity is suspended.
  • The AI's confidence is below the threshold.
  • A credit check falls in the high-risk tier.

This page describes a prototype. Thresholds are example values, not real policy, and nothing here is NIST certification.

Accessibility statement

This service aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What you can do

  • Use every feature with a keyboard. A "Skip to main content" link is the first thing on the page.
  • Make text larger or smaller with the A− A A+ buttons at the top. Zoom up to 400% also works.
  • Switch to high contrast colours with the button at the top.
  • Use a screen reader: pages have landmarks and headings, and new answers are announced.
  • Reduce motion: animations stop if your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Write requests in English, Hindi or Hinglish.

Known limitations

  • The website text is in English only.
  • Wide tables scroll sideways on small screens.

Privacy

The site loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. With "Rules only" or "Local AI", nothing you type leaves the server you are using. With "Cloud AI", your message is sent to Groq to be interpreted; your records never are.
